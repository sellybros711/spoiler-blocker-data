Plot Armor: Spoiler Blocker for Movies & TV privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Plot Armor: Spoiler Blocker for Movies & TV works entirely on your computer. It does not collect, sell or share your data, and it does not send anything you read or do to anyone.

What Plot Armor reads

Plot Armor only runs on x.com and twitter.com. On those pages it reads the posts on your screen (their words, date, author name, link previews and photo descriptions) and the trending topics, so it can decide whether to cover each one. This happens inside your browser, on your computer. Post text is never saved and never sent anywhere.

What Plot Armor saves, and where

Everything is saved in your browser's own storage on your computer:

  • the shows you track and the last episode you have watched of each;
  • your settings (how covers look, "watching live tonight", questions you have answered);
  • a copy of the show lists (names, keywords and episode dates);
  • words Plot Armor learned automatically from posts about a show you track (only the words themselves, never the posts), which expire after 48 hours;
  • how many posts it covered this week and last week (just the number, never which posts), shown in the popup.

There is no account, no sign-in and no Plot Armor server. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

What Plot Armor downloads

Once a day, when your browser starts, and once an hour while a show you track is airing, Plot Armor downloads the latest show lists (episode dates, cast names and keywords) from sellybros711.github.io/spoiler-blocker-data, a public website hosted by GitHub. This is a plain download, like opening a web page: nothing about you or your shows is sent. As with any website, GitHub can see your IP address when the files are downloaded. See GitHub's privacy statement.

This is the only connection Plot Armor makes to the internet.

Requesting a show, reporting a problem, and removing Plot Armor

The "Request a show" and "Report a problem" links in Plot Armor open forms on Google Forms in a new tab, and when you remove Plot Armor, Chrome opens a short "Why did you remove it?" form. Plot Armor itself sends nothing; you choose what to type and whether to submit it. We use what you send only to decide which shows to add and what to fix, and, if you leave an email address, to reply to you. We do not share it or use it for anything else. Google's privacy policy covers the form itself. To have a request deleted, email us at the address below.

What Plot Armor does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of data, and no use of data for anything except covering spoilers.
  • No reading of other websites, your messages, or your X account details.
  • No posting, liking or anything else on X on your behalf.

Changes and contact

Plot Armor is not affiliated with X, or with any show, network or wrestling company it covers.
